# VexBot Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** Sept 2026  
**Operator:** Vextryl  

This Privacy Policy explains how VexBot processes information when it records
and transcribes Discord voice channels. VexBot is a self-hosted Discord
application. The person or organization operating a particular VexBot instance
is responsible for that instance's storage, configuration, and data practices.

## 1. Information VexBot processes

VexBot processes only the information needed to record, transcribe, deliver,
and administer its features. This may include:

- Discord user IDs and display names for people whose audio is received;
- Discord server, channel, and interaction identifiers;
- voice-channel audio recordings, stored in separate files for each speaker;
- timestamps, recording metadata, and chronological transcripts;
- commands and status information needed to operate the bot; and
- limited technical logs, such as connection, error, storage, and recording
  events.

VexBot receives this information from Discord and from participants' audio in a
voice channel. It does not ask for Discord passwords, account tokens, payment
information, or other login credentials.

## 2. How VexBot uses information

VexBot uses this information to:

- join a requested voice channel and record audio;
- create local transcripts and identify speakers where Discord information is
  available;
- post recording status and completed transcripts to the configured Discord
  channel;
- maintain configured recording retention, troubleshoot errors, and protect
  the service; and
- comply with applicable legal obligations.

VexBot does not sell personal information, use recordings or Discord data for
advertising, or use them to train artificial-intelligence models.

## 3. Recording notice and consent

Recording voice audio may require notice or consent under applicable law. The
server owner and the person who starts a recording are responsible for giving
participants clear notice and obtaining any required consent before VexBot
begins recording.

VexBot is not intended for secret recording or unlawful surveillance. See the
[VexBot Terms of Service](TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md) for additional responsibilities
and use restrictions.

## 4. Storage and retention

In VexBot's standard configuration, audio recordings, transcripts, and related
metadata are stored on the server selected by the VexBot operator. Transcription
is performed locally using the operator's installed transcription software.

The operator configures how many completed recording sessions to retain. The
standard configuration retains five completed sessions. The operator may change
that setting, delete recordings manually, or maintain backups that are subject
to separate retention practices.

## 5. Sharing and disclosures

VexBot shares information only as needed to provide its features:

- with Discord, when VexBot sends messages, status updates, or transcripts to a
  Discord channel;
- with the server operator and people who have access to the Discord channels,
  host storage, or backups configured by that operator;
- with service providers used by an operator to host, secure, or maintain an
  instance, subject to that operator's arrangements with those providers; and
- when required by applicable law, a lawful legal process, or to protect the
  security of VexBot, its users, or others.

VexBot's standard configuration does not send voice recordings to a cloud
transcription provider.

## 6. Security

The operator should use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the instance,
including restricting host and Discord-channel access, protecting the Discord
token, securing backups, and keeping the host and software updated. No storage
or transmission method is completely secure, and VexBot cannot guarantee
absolute security.

## 7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction,
deletion, restriction, or portability of personal information, or to object to
certain processing. To make a request, contact the operator listed above.

If VexBot is hosted by a server owner or organization other than Vextryl,
contact that host first. They control the relevant recording files, transcripts,
retention settings, and backups. The operator may need to verify a requester's
identity and may retain information where permitted or required by law.

## 8. International processing

VexBot data is processed where the operator's host, backups, and Discord
services are located. These locations may be outside your state, province, or
country and may have different privacy protections.

## 9. Children's privacy

VexBot is not directed to children under 13 or the minimum age required to use
Discord in their jurisdiction. Do not use VexBot in a way that knowingly
collects personal information from people who are not permitted to use Discord.

## 10. Changes to this policy

The operator may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to VexBot or
applicable law. The effective date above identifies the current version.
Continued use of VexBot after an update means the updated policy applies.

## 11. Contact

For questions, privacy requests, or concerns, contact Vextryl.
